♻️ Pass the Book, Not the Trash

Read, Share, Reforest.

RE:READ Community is designed with a dual purpose: directly preserving trees by giving physical books endless life, while helping readers rebuild deep focus, form tangible reading habits, and step away from constant digital glare for a meaningful screen detox.

🎯 Our Mission & UN SDG Alignment

Why we pass physical books instead of buying new ones.

🌲 Why 25 Trees Goal?

Standard paper production consumes massive amounts of timber. Since 1 tree yields roughly 25 books, recirculating physical copies saves entire mini-forests from being cut down for single-use reading.

📱 Digital Detox & Habit Forming

Physical reading restores cognitive attention spans destroyed by screen overexposure. RE:READ turns physical book handoffs into a habit-forming community ritual.

🇺🇳 Global Goals

Our community directly advances United Nations Sustainable Development Goals by promoting sustainable consumption habits and preserving forest ecosystems.

SDG 12: Responsible Consumption SDG 15: Life on Land
