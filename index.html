Our Core Principles

Simple guidelines that keep our book-sharing eco-system thriving.

📖 Keep Books Moving

Once you finish reading a book, pass it along to a friend, drop it at a local hub, or list it back on the shelf within 30 days.

🤝 Zero Obligation

Borrowing, donating, or acknowledging our project is entirely optional. Explore and engage at whatever level feels right for you.

🌍 Measurable Eco-Impact

By reusing physical copies instead of buying single-use prints, we collectively reduce pulp production and save water.
