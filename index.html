♻️ Pass the Book, Not the Trash

Read, Share, Reforest.

Physical books are meant to be read, not forgotten on shelves or thrown away. We are building an open peer-to-peer circulation network where every shared page turns into real-world environmental impact.

🎯 Our Mission & UN SDG Alignment

Why we pass physical books instead of buying new ones.

🌲 Why 25 Trees?

Standard book production uses massive amounts of virgin timber and water. By recirculating physical copies, our goal is to save the equivalent of 25 mature trees from being felled for single-read books.

♻️ Impact of Reusing

Reusing 1 physical book 5 times saves up to 12 lbs of CO2 emissions and conserves over 1,400 gallons of water compared to producing 5 new standalone paperbacks.

🇺🇳 Global Goals

Our community directly advances United Nations Sustainable Development Goals by promoting sustainable consumption habits and preserving forest ecosystems.
