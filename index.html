🌳 25 Trees Target

Global Impact Context & Gen-Z Call to Action

🪓 The Deforestation Reality

Human activity cuts down over 15 billion trees globally every single year. Paper production alone accounts for billions of consumed trees annually. Recirculating physical books directly stems this loss at the grass-roots level.

🇺🇳 UN Sustainable Development Goals

Our 25-Tree Target directly aligns with UN SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption) and SDG 15 (Life on Land) by maximizing the life cycle of every printed volume before new paper needs to be milled.

📱 Digital Detox vs. Screen Addiction

Gen Z is often labeled as digital-chocolics hooked on endless feed scrolling. By choosing physical paperbacks, we prove that our generation can unplug, reclaim deep focus, and lead circular eco-movements.
