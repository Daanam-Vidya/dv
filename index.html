🌱 Core Principles

Circular Reuse

Extending item lifespan to conserve natural resources and minimize waste.

Mindful Reading

Fostering deliberate, tactile engagement with literature over screen distraction.

Privacy First

Zero tracking, account setups, or data collection — just standard correspondence.

🌳 25 Trees
2-Year Target: Conserve the paper equivalent of 25 mature trees over the next 2 years through active book recirculation. A clear, achievable milestone built step-by-step with every shared reading cycle.
⚡ Environmental Impact Framework
First-Mile Calculations: Metrics account exclusively for direct primary distributions from this shelf. As readers continuously pass titles onward, the effective ecological benefit multiplies further.

📖 How to borrow
1. Select a Title

Explore available catalog items and review their descriptions.

2. Request via Email

Click "Request Book" to launch a pre-formatted message with delivery details.

3. Receive & Share

The book is sent directly at no charge. Read and pass it forward when completed.

Questions or contributions?

Inquiries, recommendations, or general contact are processed directly via standard mail.
